# Survey Questionnaire (Template)

**Study:** E-commerce Customer Purchase Behavior and Satisfaction Analysis  
**Estimated time:** 3–5 minutes  
**Audience:** Adults (18+) who purchased online within the past six months.

## Participant information
You are invited to participate in an educational survey about online shopping experiences. Participation is voluntary. You may skip questions and stop before submitting. Do not provide your name, contact information, address, account credentials, order number, or payment details. Responses are intended for the stated research purpose and aggregate reporting. Add the researcher/instructor's actual contact information before fielding.

This template is not a substitute for ethics review where required.

## Consent
1. I am at least 18 and have read the information above.
   - Yes, I consent
   - No, I do not consent (end survey)

## Eligibility
2. Have you purchased from an e-commerce platform in the past six months?
   - Yes
   - No (end survey)

## Shopping behavior
3. How often do you typically purchase online?
   - Less than monthly
   - 1–2 times per month
   - 3–5 times per month
   - 6 or more times per month

## Satisfaction
Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neither agree nor disagree, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly agree.

4. Overall, I am satisfied with my recent online shopping experience. (1–5)
5. Product information was accurate and helpful. (1–5)
6. The checkout process was convenient. (1–5)
7. The delivery experience met my expectations. (1–5)

## Repeat-purchase intention
8. I intend to purchase from this platform again. (1–5)
9. I would consider this platform for my next online purchase. (1–5)
10. I would recommend this platform to someone I know. (1–5)

## Optional demographic
11. Age band:
   - 18–24
   - 25–34
   - 35–44
   - 45–54
   - 55+
   - Prefer not to say

## Closing
Thank you. Avoid including identifying information in free text. The practice survey intentionally has no free-text field.
